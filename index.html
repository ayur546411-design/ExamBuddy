Everything you need to navigate your semesters at Guru Ghasidas Vishwavidyalaya (A Central University, Bilaspur) without the chaos.

💡 ExamBuddy Kya Hai?

ExamBuddy ek dedicated, student-centric academic platform hai jo Guru Ghasidas Vishwavidyalaya (GGU) ke har student ke exam preparation ko aasan aur organized banane ke liye design kiya gaya hai.

Chahe aap 1st year freshers hon ya final year graduating student, ExamBuddy aapko authentic academic materials, real-time university notifications aur smart grading tools single tap par provide karta hai.

  • ✓ 100% GGU Syllabus & Grading Ordinance alignment
  • ✓ Clean, ad-friendly UI built for fast revision
  • ✓ Regular updates from verified class representatives and toppers

🎯 GGU Students Ke Liye Kya Solve Karta Hai?

University life mein study materials aur crucial updates dhoondhna sabse badi headache hoti hai. ExamBuddy in sabhi pain-points ko जड़ se khatam karta hai:

  • ✕ Scattered Study Material: WhatsApp & Telegram groups mein notes kho jaane ka jhanjhat khatam.
  • ✕ Missing Official Circulars: Exam date-sheets, re-evaluation aur fee circulars miss hone ka dar nahi.
  • ✕ Outdated Question Papers: Photocopier shop par line lagaye bina authentic PYQs download karein.
  • ✕ Attendance Shortage Surprise: 75% rule ki tension ko real-time attendance calculator se control karein.
