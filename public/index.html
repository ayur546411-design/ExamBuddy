About ExamBuddy

Built for university life, with students in mind

ExamBuddy brings university-specific academic resources and practical exam preparation tools together. Its current resource catalog focuses on Guru Ghasidas Vishwavidyalaya (GGU), with a vision to support more universities over time.

💡 One academic companion

ExamBuddy is an all-in-one academic resource and exam preparation platform for university students. It brings study materials and useful student tools together in one place.

Resources are organized around the academic structure they belong to, helping students find relevant materials by institution, department, semester, and subject. The currently available catalog is focused on GGU.

  • ✓ Current catalog of GGU academic resources
  • ✓ Study resources and student tools in one place
  • ✓ A platform designed to grow to more universities over time

🎯 Make academic resources easier to find

University resources can be spread across many places. ExamBuddy brings available materials and useful tools together so students can find what they need with less searching:

  • ✕ Scattered Study Material: WhatsApp & Telegram groups mein notes kho jaane ka jhanjhat khatam.
  • ✕ Scattered study material: Keep available notes and question papers easier to find.
  • ✕ Hard-to-find academic updates: Bring available notices and important dates into one study companion.
  • ✕ Everyday study planning: Use practical tools to estimate grades and track attendance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about resources currently available and the platform's plans.

The currently available ExamBuddy resources and website tools are free to access. The academic catalog currently focuses on GGU; resource availability can vary by course and subject.
The current catalog includes question papers from past GGU mid-term and end-term examinations. Papers are organized with their available subject and year information. Always check official university sources for authoritative materials.
The web calculator uses a 10-point grade scale (O = 10, A+ = 9, A = 8, B+ = 7, B = 6, C = 5, P = 4, F = 0) intended for the current GGU CBCS resource catalog. Check your university's official ordinance before relying on a calculated result.
You can ask about contributing relevant study materials by emailing office@exambuddy.info. Include the university, department, semester, subject, and source details so the team can review the request.
University selection is not currently available on this website. The published academic resource catalog focuses on GGU; expanding to more universities is part of the platform's future vision. We will only list additional institutions as their resources become available.
ExamBuddy is an independent, non-official educational resource app developed by student developers and alumni to assist the student community. For official university administration, always refer to ggu.ac.in.
