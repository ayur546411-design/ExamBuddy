Made for GGU students

A team that understands student life.

ExamBuddy brings useful study resources and everyday academic tools together, shaped around the needs of Guru Ghasidas Vishwavidyalaya students.

Built around one shared goal

We are a student-led effort, with GGU students and alumni helping curate resources and improve the experience. Our focus is simple: make exam preparation more organized, accessible, and less stressful.

📚

Academic resources

Syllabus, previous year questions, notes, notices, and results are brought together to make important study material easier to find.

🛠️

Product & technology

The app and website are designed to keep study resources and practical tools, like grade and attendance calculators, easy to use.

Student community
